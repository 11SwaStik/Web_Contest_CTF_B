Frequently asked questions

How do I change my plan?

Go to Billing → Change plan. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades at the next cycle.

Where can I find my invoices?

All invoices are under Billing. Click any invoice to view or download it.

How do I invite a teammate?

Open Team → Invite member and enter their email. They'll receive an invitation.

How do I reset my password?

Go to Settings → Change password. You'll need your current password.

What if I forget my password?

Use the Forgot your password? link on the sign-in page. Enter your username and we'll send a reset link to the email on file (in this demo environment, the link is shown directly on the page instead of emailed).

Still need help?

Email support@fieldnote.example — we reply within one business day.
